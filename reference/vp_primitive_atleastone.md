# The at-least-one lines from which all others follow

The primitive at-least-one lines: those that do not follow from another
one. "Every product has f or g" and "g requires h" give "every product
has f or h": at-least-one lines are inherited upwards, the mirror image
of conflicts. The same as the file \_\_atleastone_primitive.net of
Strong4VM (vp_generate(or_graph = TRUE)).

## Usage

``` r
vp_primitive_atleastone(model)
```

## Arguments

- model:

  A model, from
  [`vp_generate()`](https://rheradio.github.io/variplex/reference/vp_generate.md)
  or
  [`vp_read()`](https://rheradio.github.io/variplex/reference/vp_read.md),
  in its complete or its minimal form.

## Value

A tibble with `from` and `to` (atomic sets as their representative).
